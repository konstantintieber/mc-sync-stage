## Abstract

Most enterprises face the same tension: give people the freedom to automate and you risk losing control; lock everything down and nothing moves. GE Vernova, a global business which helps generate a significant share of the world's electricity, is charting a path through the middle. In this fireside chat, Senior Enterprise Architect Ravi Karnam and Senior GenAI Product Manager Susan Moore share how a large, safety-conscious industrial company is enabling engineers and non-engineers alike to build real automations; all while holding a clear line on governance, risk, and where AI does and doesn't belong. Expect a candid, practical conversation about executive buy-in, guardrails that enable rather than restrict, and the culture change that makes it all stick.

## Takeaway

A practical model for "governed democratisation": how to let many people build without creating chaos or risk.

How to define your automation "danger zones," and why designing for human review matters when AI is never 100% right.

Why the most valuable automations often use no AI at all and how to tell the difference.

What it actually takes to shift behaviour and sustain momentum after the initial excitement fades.
