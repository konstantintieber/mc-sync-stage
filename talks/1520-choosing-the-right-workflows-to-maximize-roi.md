# Choosing the Right Workflows to Maximize ROI

- **When:** Wed 14 Oct 2026, 15:20–15:50 (30 min)
- **Where:** Sync Stage (Studio 3), Ecosystem Day
- **Format:** Talk
- **MC:** Aya
- **Detail page:** https://n8n.io/intheloop/agenda/choosing-the-right-workflows-to-maximize-roi-ff61a306/

## Abstract

Plenty of agentic workflows work. Far fewer can be proven to work in terms the business recognizes. That gap is what stalls automation programs after the first wave: the results are real, but nobody set up the measurement to show it, so the next round of investment never gets approved.

We'll share the AI value radar: a six-step framework for choosing opportunities that are set up from the start to demonstrate their value, scored across safety, feasibility, financial impact, and clear ownership. You'll see how to pick workflows where the baseline is knowable and the outcome is attributable, and how to measure them in a way that lands with a CFO rather than only with the team that built them.

Technology is only the visible tip of the transformation. Ownership, operating model, and how you define success decide whether the rest of the value can be seen at all.

## Takeaway

Prioritize agentic workflows for provability, not just feasibility: know the baseline before you build

Apply a six-step value radar scored across safety, feasibility, financial impact, and named ownership

Measure in terms the business already uses, so results land with finance and not only with the build team

Understand why freeing up people's time no longer counts, and why real value usually needs both structural cost reduction and better output quality

Spot the workflows that look promising on paper but can never be attributed to a business outcome
